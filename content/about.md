**About me**

Hi! I'm Fernando. I usually spend my time trying to understand why the computer is not behaving properly - at least when I'm not playing Pokémon cards with my kids.

I work as a platform engineer, which means I write software and break the infrastructure. I'm interested in the linux kernel (especially memory management and I/O), runtimes, databases, and production systems. 

Send me a message  at frn@frn.sh or [linkedin](https://linkedin.com/in/frnsimoes).

**About the blog**

I try to keep the format loose. These are war stories, production notes, and software archeology posts. This is also where I [procrastinate](https://www.structuredprocrastination.com/). If you are interested, subscribe via [RSS](/index.xml).
