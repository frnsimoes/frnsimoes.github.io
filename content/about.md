My name is Fernando Simões. I grew up in the 90s. My first computer was an [Intel 486](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SUvlWt9WTKA) with Windows 95. I probably installed my first Linux distro sometime between 1996 and 1998. It was a CD that came with a magazine. I was a little imp, so I used to carry the CD around and install the distro on whatever computer I encountered - like my cousin's, or the computer in the repair shop while my dad was talking to the guy and nobody was watching me. As you can imagine, that caused quite a bit of confusion.

I used to play Doom off floppy disks and hear the [dial-up beep boop](https://www.windytan.com/2012/11/the-sound-of-dialup-pictured.html) every weekend. DSL came in 2002. No more beep boop. 

I work as a platform engineer, even though I consider myself more on the [SRE side](https://rachelbythebay.com/w/2024/09/03/ops/) of things. I'm particularly interested in I/O, memory management, runtimes, databases, and, of course, performance, distributed systems, and reliability.
