**About me**

I'm Fernando Simões. I work as a platform engineer. Before I got deep into computers, I used to read a bunch of philosophy and classical literature. I have three children and spend most of my time with them when I'm not trying to desperately understand why the computer is misbehaving. 

I'm mostly interested in database internals, the linux kernel (especially memory management and I/O), runtimes, and production systems.

**About the blog**

I try to keep the format loose. These are mostly war stories and short notes. I also use this page to practice [structured procrastination](https://www.structuredprocrastination.com/).
