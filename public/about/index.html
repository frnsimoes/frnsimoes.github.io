Fernando Simões

My name is Fernando Simões. I grew up in the 90s. My first computer was an Intel 486 with Windows 95. I probably installed my first Linux distro sometime between 1999 and 2001. It was a CD that came with a magazine. I was a little imp, so I used to carry the CD around and install the distro on whatever computer I encountered - like my cousin’s, or the computer in the repair shop while my dad was talking to the guy and nobody was watching me. As you can imagine, that caused quite a bit of confusion.

I used to play Doom off floppy disks and hear the dial-up beep boop every weekend. DSL came in 2002. No more beep boop.

I currently work as a platform engineer, even though I consider myself more on the SRE side of things. I’m particularly interested in performance and reliability problems in systems that handle large volumes of data, especially problems that involve memory management, I/O, language runtimes, and databases.

Email me at about whatever - systems, career, an interesting article. I’m always up for a good conversation.
