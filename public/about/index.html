My name is Fernando Simões. I grew up in the 90s. My first computer was an Intel 486 with Windows 95. I probably installed my first Linux distro sometime between 1996 and 1998. It was a CD that came with a magazine. I was a little imp, so I used to carry the CD around and install the distro on whatever computer I encountered - like my cousin’s, or the computer in the repair shop while my dad was talking to the guy and nobody was watching me. As you can imagine, that caused quite a bit of confusion.

I used to play Doom off floppy disks and hear the dial-up beep boop every weekend. DSL came in 2002. No more beep boop.

I work as a platform engineer, even though I consider myself more on the SRE side of things. I’m particularly interested in I/O, memory management, runtimes, databases, and, of course, performance, distributed systems, and reliability.

Email me at about whatever - systems, career, an interesting article. I’m always up for a good conversation.
